The M.R. Tiny House
Standard

We believe a home should be personal. That's why every M.R. Tiny House is hand-built by Rand and his team, ensuring a level of care and quality you won't find in a prefab. These are custom-crafted residences built for real people, not just units off an assembly line.

Extreme Snow Load

Engineered for a 60 lb PSF rating—designed to easily handle over 4 feet of solid Idaho snowpack.

Structural Insulation

2lb closed-cell spray foam adds immense rigidity to the frame and superior thermal protection.

Full Utility Hookups

Standard 50 Amp electrical, 3" PVC sewer drains, and 20-gallon water heaters come standard.

The "Shell" Option

Save money by having us handle the structural framing and rough-ins while you finish the interior.
